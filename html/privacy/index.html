    Privacy Policy

    Effective date: May 16, 2026

    Luna is designed as an educational space app that keeps personal app preferences simple and local wherever possible. This policy explains what the app and this website handle.

    1. App Data Stored On Your Device

    Luna stores app preferences on your device, including onboarding choices, appearance, haptics, preferred view mode, scale settings, labels, orbit guides, and related experience controls.

    The celestial body catalog is bundled as local JSON. It includes public educational data such as names, summaries, facts, orbit values, tags, texture names, and model names.

    2. ARKit And Device Capabilities

    When you use AR mode, Luna uses Apple ARKit to understand the local scene enough to place and display space content. AR processing is handled by the device and Luna does not run its own external AR mapping service.

    3. NASA Astronomy Picture Of The Day

    Luna includes NASA Astronomy Picture of the Day. When this feature loads current APOD content, the app may request APOD metadata and media from NASA services. NASA may receive standard request information such as IP address, device network details, and request timing according to NASA's own service practices.

    4. Website Analytics

    This website uses Swetrix analytics through NovaPro's analytics host to understand basic page traffic. The no-script analytics endpoint is hosted at analytics.novapro.net. Website analytics is separate from Luna's in-app experience.

    5. NASA Assets And Credits

    Luna uses NASA/JPL imagery, NASA Scientific Visualization Studio imagery, and NASA 3D assets for educational visualization. Credits and source links are included in the app. Luna is independent and is not endorsed by NASA.

    6. Contact

    Questions about Luna or this policy can be sent to daniel@dkravec.net.
