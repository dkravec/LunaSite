    App Store overview

    An immersive space exploration experience built for iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

    Place planets into your room with AR, explore the solar system in interactive 3D scenes, and discover daily NASA imagery, space facts, and celestial objects across the universe.

    Designed with a modern native Apple experience, Luna Explorer combines education, visualization, and exploration into a clean and interactive experience.

    Whether you want to casually explore space or dive deeper into the solar system, Luna Explorer makes discovering the universe interactive and accessible.
