    Support

    Need help with Luna? This page covers common setup and troubleshooting notes for AR, visual mode, body data, and APOD.

    Quick Start

    • Open Explore to browse planets, moons, satellites, and other bodies from Luna's local catalog.
    • Open Experience to view the solar system scene, show orbit guides, and play orbit motion.
    • Open a body detail page and choose object mode to focus on one body at a time.
    • Use AR mode on supported iPhone and iPad devices, or visual mode when AR is unavailable.

    Troubleshooting

    • AR is unavailable: Confirm your device supports ARKit world tracking and that camera permission is allowed.
    • Placement is difficult: Move slowly, point the camera at a textured surface, and use good lighting before placing the scene.
    • The scene feels too spread out: Change scaling preferences to educational or compressed distance mode.
    • Objects are not moving: Check orbit playback speed and object rotation speed in the experience controls.
    • Body data is missing: Restart the app so Luna can reload the bundled JSON catalog.
    • APOD does not load: Check your internet connection. NASA APOD content is fetched from NASA services and may depend on their availability.
